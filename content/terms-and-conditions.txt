2. Monthly amount for each role
Interagood charges a small monthly amount to keep your business card online. The amount depends on your role:
- Supplier: Rs 219 per month
- Partner: Rs 109 per month
3. Why this amount is charged
The amount is charged only to maintain your profile and business card and to bring you orders and enquiries from clients.
- There are no hidden charges.
- There is no commission on the work you get or on the money you earn.
- Once you make the payment, your business card is activated and stays online for that month.
4. No fixed subscription
There is no compulsory subscription. Every month you will be notified when your payment is due. If you pay, your business card stays online and you keep receiving orders. If you do not want to continue, you can skip the payment. The choice is always yours.
5. No refund
Payments made to Interagood are not refundable. Please pay only when you are ready to keep your business card online.
6. Your app after registration
When your registration is complete you get your Interagood app. From the app you can manage your whole system: your business profile, your business card and the samples of your work. Uploading good samples improves your chances of getting the right work.
7. Deleting your account
You can delete your account at any time from the app. When an account is deleted, your profile, business card and samples are removed from Interagood.
8. Your responsibilities
- Give correct information about yourself and your work.

- Treat clients with honesty and respect and deliver what you promise.
- Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
