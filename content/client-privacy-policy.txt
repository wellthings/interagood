Interagood Privacy Policy
Platform: Interagood (interagood.in) | Last Updated: November 2026
At Interagood, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect
- Account & Contact Information: Your name, phone number, email address, and login credentials provided during signup.
- Project & Location Details: Site address, geocoded location data, service category selections, and project descriptions/images uploaded to process your order.

2. How We Use Your Information
- Order Processing & Matching: To evaluate your project scale and connect you with the most suitable Service Partner, Architect, or Supplier.
- Platform Communication: To send order updates, assignment notifications, and service-related support.
- Service Improvement: To analyze platform usage and improve our future offerings and user experience.

3. Data Sharing & Security
- Controlled Sharing: Your contact and project details are shared only with the specific Service Partner or Architect assigned to your request, or with Suppliers you explicitly choose to contact.
- No Data Selling: Interagood does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to unauthorized third parties or marketing agencies.
- Data Protection: We implement industry-standard security protocols to safeguard your data against unauthorized access or misuse.

4. Your Rights
- You may review, update, or request the deletion of your account and personal information at any time by contacting our support team.
